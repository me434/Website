What I built

I was responsible for building everything from the ground up: the brand identity, website, quote and buy journey, price presentation, payment flow and the app.

  • All supporting materials, including emails, policy documents, photography and videography.
  • After launch, Ticker evolved constantly. I made sure the product delivered an exceptional experience while managing stakeholder expectations.
  • As the business scaled rapidly, I focused on keeping everything scalable and future-proof, leading to a comprehensive design system that kept consistency across the board.
