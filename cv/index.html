Recent experience in brief

I’m Head of Design at Just Insure, a US telematics motor insurtech. I lead design across the whole business, from everything customer facing to all our internal tools, as we launch across 8 states.

I’ve built AI into almost every part of the design process. It helps us work faster, make the customer journey smoother, and back our design decisions with data. I’m also setting up the systems that let design keep working well as the company gets bigger.

Before this, I was Senior Product Designer at Gaia, the world’s first IVF insurer and fintech, with policies in the UK and USA. There, I worked closely with design advisor Chris Linnett, known for leading the launch of MSN Messenger and for Design Director roles at Spotify, Deliveroo, Typeform, Nokia and more.

I work on every part of the product process, from early ideation and user research to high-fidelity prototypes. I work closely with designers, copywriters, product managers, engineers and senior stakeholders, and I care about building design that holds up as the company grows.
