Key responsibilities

I lead design at Just, a car insurance company, as it grows and expands into new US states. I set the long-term vision and roadmap for the design department, and I make sure design decisions come from real user insight and deliver measurable business results, mainly profitability, conversion and retention.

  • Strategy: I set the design direction around two goals, profitability and scalability, and share it with leadership.
  • User research: I built the research practice from the ground up, including customer interviews, surveys, A/B testing, journey maps and an insights repository.
  • Customer experience: I improve the full customer journey, from quote and purchase through to renewals and in-app engagement.
  • Platforms and systems: I own the design system and the internal operations platform, including the Agent Portal for rolling out to new states.
  • Brand: I’m leading the brand’s move from start-up to trusted insurer, working with an external agency and the Marketing team.
  • Working across teams: I work with Product, Engineering, Marketing and Compliance to deliver new products and state launches.
  • Team building: I’m growing the design team and set the bar for design quality.
  • AI adoption: I’ve brought AI into every part of the design process where it adds value, from research synthesis and ideation to prototyping, content and design system work. This helps the team move faster without lowering quality.
